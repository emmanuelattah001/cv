My name is Attah Emmanuel, and I recently completed my secondary education at Shephard High School in July 2024. After graduation, I was determined to advance my knowledge in technology, a field that has always captivated me. In September 2024, I enrolled in a three-month web development training program at Sabi Programmer, where I gained foundational skills in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript etc. This training not only solidified my passion for technology but also inspired me to pursue a degree in computer science to deepen my knowledge and create impactful solutions for real-world challenges. FAMILY BACKGROUND Growing up, my journey was marked by resilience and determination. In 2015, I lost both my parents, and since then, I have lived with my elder brother, Mr. Attah Nathaniel Dominic, who has been my primary source of support and inspiration. Despite our limited financial resources, my brother has encouraged me to strive for excellence and to use education as a means to improve our lives and the lives of others around us. These experiences have fueled my desire to succeed and to give back to my community. HOW I HAVE BEEN IMPACTING MY COMMUNITY I have been impactfully contributing to my community through my church CRS (Christian Community Service) and recently I was involved in organizing and coordinating the 2024 Teenagers Tech Boot Camp for the RCCG Ondo Province 3 Teenager Department. Held on December 20, 2024, this event provided teenagers with an introduction to technology and its potential for personal and community development. Using the skills I acquired during my web development training, I assisted in planning the event, mentoring participants, and teaching basi c web development concepts. Witnessing the enthusiasm and curiosity of the attendees reaffirmed my belief in the transformative power of technology and education. This experience also highlighted the importance of empowering young people with the tools and knowledge they need to succeed in a digital world. HOW MY SKILL AND COURSE OF STUDY ALIEN My previous skills in web development align seamlessly with my aspiration to study computer science at the college level. The training I received not only introduced me to the technical aspects of programming but also taught me problem-solving, collaboration, and project management—skills that are essential in the field of computer science. By building on this foundation, I hope to explore advanced topics such as software engineering, artificial intelligence, and data science, which will enable me to develop innovative solutions to complex problems. The transition from my prior experience to a comprehensive computer science program represents a natural progression that will allow me to maximize my potential and broaden my impact.
